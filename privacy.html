Privacy Policy — M310 Land Works

Effective date: July 11, 2026

Last updated: July 11, 2026

M310 Land Works ("M310," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, and the choices you have. It applies to information collected through www.m310landworks.com, our Facebook and Instagram lead forms, phone calls, text messages, and any other channel where we interact with you.

By using our website or submitting a request, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Who We Are

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you give us directly and information collected automatically when you interact with our website or ads.

Information you give us:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address or approximate location
  • Description of the work you need (service type, property size, timing)
  • Any other information you choose to share in the message field of a form, over the phone, or by text

Information collected automatically:

  • Device information (browser type, operating system, IP address)
  • Website usage information (pages visited, time on page, referring source)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 7)
  • Facebook Pixel and Conversions API events (page views, lead-form submissions, button clicks)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Contact you about your service request, including by phone call, SMS text message, and email
  • Provide a free on-site estimate and follow up on quoted work
  • Schedule, perform, and follow up on services
  • Respond to your questions
  • Send you updates about your project or account
  • Improve our website, ads, and services
  • Measure the performance of our advertising (including Facebook and Instagram ads)
  • Prevent fraud, enforce our terms, and comply with the law

We do not sell your personal information.

4. SMS / Text Message Communications

By submitting your phone number through our website, a Facebook/Instagram lead form, or over the phone, you consent to receive text messages from M310 Land Works at that number, including messages related to your quote, appointment, project status, and follow-up.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for help.
  • Opting out of SMS does not affect our ability to contact you by phone or email.

Your mobile phone number and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

5. When We Share Information

We share your information only in these situations:

  • Service providers who work on our behalf — such as our CRM (Zoho), scheduling software, call-tracking, email/SMS delivery, and payment processors. These providers are contractually required to use your information only to perform services for us.
  • Advertising partners — we use Meta (Facebook/Instagram) advertising tools, including the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, which may receive limited information (hashed email or phone, event data) so we can measure ad performance and reach similar audiences. You can control ad personalization in your Facebook Ad Settings.
  • Legal reasons — if required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of M310, our customers, or the public.
  • Business transfer — if M310 is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third-party affiliates for their independent marketing.

6. Facebook / Instagram Lead Ads

If you submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta collects your responses and shares them with M310 so we can contact you about your request. Meta's use of your information is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy. Our use of that information is governed by this policy.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies (including the Meta Pixel and, where applicable, Google Analytics) to:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Measure site traffic
  • Understand how ads perform
  • Show relevant ads to you on Facebook, Instagram, and other platforms

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the site.

8. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide services, communicate with you about active or past work, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 10).

9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted over the internet.

10. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • Access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by contacting us at the number or address below
  • Opt out of marketing emails by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email
  • Opt out of SMS by replying STOP to any text
  • Limit ad tracking through your device or Facebook Ad Settings

Residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and other states with applicable privacy laws may have additional rights, including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion, the right to correct inaccuracies, the right to opt out of "sales" or "sharing" of personal information (we do not sell), and the right to be free from discrimination for exercising these rights. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 12.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services and website are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.
