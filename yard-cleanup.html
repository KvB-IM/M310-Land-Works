Common Questions

What does a yard cleanup include?

Clearing the overgrowth, brush piles, fallen limbs and accumulated debris that have built up until the yard is no longer usable or mowable. It is aimed at properties that have got away from you — after a long absence, a rental turnover, or a season nobody kept up with.

Can you clean up a property that has been neglected for years?

Yes, and that is most of this work. Heavy overgrowth is past what a mower or a homeowner with a trimmer can reasonably take on. Tracked equipment handles it in a fraction of the time and without the injury risk.

Do you haul the debris away?

Yes, we can haul the material off so you are not left with piles to deal with. Where mulching on site makes more sense — and it is often cheaper — we will say so at the estimate.

Do you clean up rental and vacant properties for landlords?

Yes. Vacant lots, rental turnovers and properties being prepared for sale or inspection are routine. We can work from an address if you are not local to meet us.

How much does yard cleanup cost?

It comes down to the size of the area, how much material there is and whether it is being hauled away or mulched. Every job starts with a free on-site estimate. We come out, walk the property, and give you a straight number with no obligation.
